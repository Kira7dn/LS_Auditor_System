# Introduction

<a id="cbam_importers_introduction_2_1_about_this_document"></a>
### INTRODUCTION 2.1 About this document

This document has been written to support stakeholders by explaining the requirements of the CBAM Regulation in a non-legislative language. This guidance focuses on the

<a id="cbam_importers_requirements_for_importers_of_cbam_goods_into"></a>
### requirements for importers of CBAM goods into the EU for the transitional period, from 1 October 2023 to 31 December 2025, during which time the CBAM is applied

without a financial obligation for importers and solely for data collection purposes.

<a id="cbam_importers_chapter_3_provides_a_quick_guidance_for_the_i"></a>
### • Chapter 3 provides a quick guidance for the intended reader of this document, the

importer of CBAM goods and/ or reporting declarant. It gives a roadmap to the most important concepts of CBAM reporting and where to find more information in this document.

<a id="cbam_importers_chapter_4_provides_an_introduction_to_the_cba"></a>
### • Chapter 4 provides an introduction to the CBAM and an overview of the

compliance cycle, roles and responsibilities and milestones and deadlines for reporting declarants during the transitional period.

<a id="cbam_importers_chapter_5_presents_an_overview_of_the_goods_a"></a>
### • Chapter 5 presents an overview of the goods and value chains for the sectors and

goods that are included in the scope of the CBAM.

<a id="cbam_importers_chapter_6_sets_out_the_reporting_obligations"></a>
### • Chapter 6 sets out the reporting obligations and recommendations which are

potentially applicable to any affected importer of CBAM goods.
•  Chapter 7 explains the general exemptions from the CBAM.
A separate guidance document is provided by the European Commission for third country operators of installations producing CBAM goods (herein referred to as “operators”). The guidance documents are accompanied by an electronic template for information that may be used by installation operators to communicate information on the embedded emissions of their goods to the reporting declarants.

<a id="cbam_importers_presentation_of_numbers_in_eu_documents"></a>
### Presentation of numbers in EU documents

To align with EU legal documents, this guidance document uses the following convention when presenting numbers.
The decimal separator used to separate the integral part of a number from its fractional part is a comma, e.g.: 0,890
Thousands, and powers of 103n thereafter, are separated by a space, e.g.:
• fifteen thousand is written as 15 000
• fifteen million is written as 15 000 000

<a id="cbam_importers_2_2_how_to_use_this_document"></a>
### 2.2 How to use this document

Where article numbers are given in this document without further specification, they always refer to the CBAM Regulation1. Where the ‘Implementing Regulation’ is cited, it means the Regulation2 which sets out the detailed monitoring and reporting rules for the transitional period. For acronyms and definitions used in this document, please see Annex A and Annex B.
A series of icons are used throughout to help guide the reader:

### The CBAM Regulation

Regulation (EU) 2023/956 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 10 May 2023 establishing a carbon border adjustment mechanism.
Available from: http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/956/oj
• Article 2 – sets out the scope of the CBAM with reference to Annex I.

<a id="cbam_importers_article_3_and_annex_iv_provide_definitions_fo"></a>
### • Article 3 and Annex IV – provide definitions for common terms used in the

CBAM.

<a id="cbam_importers_articles_5_and_17_sets_out_requirements_for_a"></a>
### • Articles 5 and 17 – sets out requirements for application for the status of

authorised CBAM declarant, by importers or their indirect customs representative, to import goods, and for the authorisation by the relevant Member State. (Apply from 31 December 2024).

<a id="cbam_importers_article_10_sets_out_requirements_for_operator"></a>
### • Article 10 – sets out requirements for operator registration under the CBAM

(from 31 December 2024).

<a id="cbam_importers_article_11_requires_member_states_to_designat"></a>
### • Article 11 – requires Member States to designate a competent authority and

for the European Commission to publish the list of competent authorities and include it in the CBAM registry.

<a id="cbam_importers_articles_14_and_16_requires_the_european_comm"></a>
### • Articles 14 and 16 – requires the European Commission to establish a CBAM

registry of authorised CBAM declarants and to assign an account to each authorised declarant. (Apply from 31 December 2024).

<a id="cbam_importers_article_30_requires_the_european_commission_t"></a>
### • Article 30 – requires the European Commission to undertake a review of the

scope of the CBAM by 31 December 2024.

<a id="cbam_importers_articles_32_to_35_set_out_the_reporting_oblig"></a>
### • Articles 32 to 35 – set out the reporting obligations on EU importers in the

transitional period.
• Article 36 – sets out the dates from when the other articles start to apply.

<a id="cbam_importers_annex_i_provides_the_list_of_cbam_goods_by_in"></a>
### • Annex I – provides the list of CBAM goods by industry sector with CN code

to identify goods, and the corresponding relevant greenhouse gases.

<a id="cbam_importers_annex_iii_identifies_the_non_eu_countries_and"></a>
### • Annex III – identifies the non-EU countries and territories that are not covered

by the CBAM.

<a id="cbam_importers_annex_iv_provides_the_general_methods_for_cal"></a>
### • Annex IV – provides the general methods for calculating the embedded

emissions in goods; in section 2 for Simple Goods and in section 3 for Complex Goods.

<a id="cbam_importers_implementing_regulation_eu_20231773_commissio"></a>
### Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/1773: Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/1773, available from: http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2023/1773/oj • Article 2 and Annex II Section 1 – provide definitions for common terms

used in the CBAM and the MRV rules.

<a id="cbam_importers_article_3_provides_the_reporting_obligations"></a>
### • Article 3 – provides the reporting obligations of the reporting declarants,

including the parameters for which data is to be reported.

<a id="cbam_importers_articles_4_and_5_set_out_the_approaches_for_t"></a>
### • Articles 4 and 5 – set out the approaches for the calculation of the embedded

emissions and conditions for the use of default values.

<a id="cbam_importers_article_6_presents_the_requirements_for_repor"></a>
### • Article 6 – presents the requirements for reporting regarding inward

processing.

<a id="cbam_importers_article_7_indicates_the_information_to_be_rep"></a>
### • Article 7 – indicates the information to be reported regarding the carbon price

due.

<a id="cbam_importers_articles_8_9_and_13_relate_to_the_obligations"></a>
### • Articles 8, 9 and 13 – relate to the obligations of the reporting declarant for

submission and modification of the CBAM reports.

<a id="cbam_importers_article_16_relates_to_the_penalties_that_shal"></a>
### • Article 16 – relates to the penalties that shall be applied by Member States if

the reporting declarant has not correctly fulfilled its reporting obligations.

<a id="cbam_importers_articles_19_and_22_set_out_technical_elements"></a>
### • Articles 19 and 22 – set out technical elements of the CBAM Transitional

Registry.
• Annex I: Table 1 - CBAM Report Structure, Table 2 - Detailed information requirements in the CBAM report.

<a id="cbam_importers_annex_ii_section_2_table_1_mapping_of_cn_code"></a>
### • Annex II: Section 2, Table 1 – mapping of CN codes to the CBAM aggregated

goods categories; and Section 3 – definition of production processes for the CBAM goods categories, including system boundaries of production routes and relevant precursors.

<a id="cbam_importers_annex_iv_minimum_data_to_be_reported_by_produ"></a>
### • Annex IV: Minimum data to be reported by producers of goods (“operators”)

to importers (or reporting declarants).

<a id="cbam_importers_annexes_v_to_vii_tables_listing_data_requirem"></a>
### • Annexes V to VII: Tables listing data requirements for other reports,

including for inward processing (by importers), EORI and the National Import System.

<a id="cbam_importers_annex_viii_standard_factors_that_may_be_used"></a>
### • Annex VIII: Standard factors that may be used for the monitoring of direct

emissions.
