# Example: Solution Roadmap (Procurement Optimization)

## 1. The Intervention Thesis
By implementing a centralized price-governance module and automating BOM-based procurement checks, the factory can reduce price leakage by 80% within 6 months.

## 2. Implementation Roadmap

| Phase | Action Item | Responsibility | Timeline |
|-------|-------------|----------------|----------|
| **Phase 1: Cleanup** | Update Master Data (Prices & BOMs) | IT + Procurement | Month 1 |
| **Phase 2: Control** | Enable "Price Hard-Stop" in ERP | IT + Finance | Month 2-3|
| **Phase 3: Optimize**| Negotiate Master Agreements | Procurement | Month 4-5|
| **Phase 4: Monitor**| Monthly Leakage Dashboard | Audit Team | Month 6+ |

## 3. Projected ROI

- **Investment cost**: $15,000 (Software update + Training)
- **Annual Savings**: $85,000 (Based on current $170k leakage rate)
- **Payback Period**: 2.1 Months
- **ROI (Year 1)**: 466%
